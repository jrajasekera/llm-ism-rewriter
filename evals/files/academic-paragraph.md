Retrieval augmentation may reduce hallucination in closed-book settings, though
the effect appears to depend on the retriever's precision rather than its recall
(Okafor et al., 2024). Our results suggest that gains reported at k=10 do not
persist at k=50, which is consistent with the dilution effect described by
Ahmad and Lindqvist (2023). We did not measure latency, and the corpus was
restricted to English, so these findings may not transfer to multilingual
settings.
