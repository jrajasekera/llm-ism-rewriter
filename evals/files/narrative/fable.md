# The Heron and the Two Ponds

A heron fished a small pond where the fish were few and thin. Across the marsh lay a wide pond where, the frogs said, the fish were fat. Each morning the heron flew to the wide pond, and each morning the pike there drove her off before she had caught anything. Each evening she came home hungry and fished the small pond in the dark.

One autumn she stopped flying out. She fished the small pond from first light, when the thin fish were slow with cold, and by the first frost she was fatter than she had ever been.

A small pond fished well feeds better than a wide one fished badly.
