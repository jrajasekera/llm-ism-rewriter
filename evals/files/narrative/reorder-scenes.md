# The Letter

The letter came with the morning post. Greta read it standing at the counter while the kettle boiled. The clinic had written two lines: the second biopsy was clear, and she need not come back for a year. She folded it into her bag. Paul was looking for his keys and did not see.

* * *

At lunch she did not eat. She walked to the travel agent on Marsh Street and bought two tickets to Lisbon for the first week of May, which was more than she had spent on anything in three years. The agent asked if it was a special occasion. Greta said she had not decided yet.

* * *

Paul found the tickets that evening, on the hall table where she had left them for him to find. He came into the kitchen holding them. "Lisbon," he said. "What's happened?"

She took the letter out of her bag and gave it to him, and watched his face while he read.
