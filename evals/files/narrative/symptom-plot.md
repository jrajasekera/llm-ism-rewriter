# The Interview

Ruben's left eyelid had started to twitch on the train, somewhere past the second tunnel. It was still going when the receptionist called his name. In the interview room he tilted his head to hide it, and the angle made him misread the clock on the wall. He thought he had twenty minutes left. He had five. He was halfway through his second answer when the chairwoman thanked him for coming.

On the platform afterwards the twitch stopped. He never found out what had started it.
