# Two Keys

The sisters had not spoken since the sale of their mother's house. Ruth had wanted to keep it and Nell had needed the money, and each had said things at the solicitor's that could not be unsaid.

In March Nell wrote to say she was ill. Ruth did not answer for a week. Then she took the train, four hours, and sat in the hospital corridor for another hour before she went in.

Nell had brought something to the hospital in her handbag. It was the spare key to the old house, which she should have handed over at the sale and had not. "I couldn't," she said. Ruth opened her own purse and took out the other one.

They laughed until the nurse came. Ruth stayed until Nell was discharged, and in the summer they went together to look at the house from the road. The new people had painted the door. Neither of them minded as much as she had expected to.
