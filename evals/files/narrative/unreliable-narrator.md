# The Piano

I sold the piano because nobody played it. That is the whole reason. It stood in the front room for eleven years with a cloth over it, and a thing nobody uses is only furniture.

Dorothea says I might have asked her first. But she had not touched it since the funeral, and an instrument nobody plays is better off with someone who will play it. I told her so.

The man who bought it brought two sons to carry it. The younger one lifted the lid and played four notes, and I told him to leave it shut until he got it home. It was out of tune. Nobody had played it, which is why I sold it.

I have the cloth still. I do not know why I kept the cloth.
