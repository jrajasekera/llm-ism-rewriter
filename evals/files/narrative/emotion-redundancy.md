# Results Day

The envelope was thinner than Priya had expected. She opened it at the bus stop because she could not wait until she got home.

She had not got the place. She was disappointed. Disappointment was the word for what she felt, a heavy sense of being let down by the result. What she experienced, standing there, was the particular letdown of having hoped for something and been refused it. She folded the letter along its creases and put it in her coat pocket.

The bus came. She let it go and walked, and by the canal she began working out what she would say to her mother.
