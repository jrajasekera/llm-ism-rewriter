# The Summer Before

I should say now that my cousin Teo drowned that August, so that you do not spend this story hoping. Everyone who tells it leaves that to the end, and I have never forgiven them for it.

In June he taught me to dive off the second pier. He counted for me, one, two, and on three he always went first, so that I would see it was safe. In July he got a job at the boatyard and came home smelling of varnish, and he let me sand the hull of a dinghy he was not supposed to touch.

On the last day of July he said that next summer he would teach me to sail. I said next summer was a long way off. He said it would come round faster than I thought.
