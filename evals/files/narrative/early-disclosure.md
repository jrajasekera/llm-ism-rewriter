# The Missing Float

It was Carol from the bakery counter who took the forty pounds from the till, and she took it for her son's school trip.

Mr Haddad noticed the shortage on Friday evening. He counted the float twice. He did not like to think it of anyone, so he began by suspecting himself, and went back through the week's receipts for a mistake in his own hand. There was none.

On Monday he watched the counter. Jamal gave the right change every time. The Saturday girl did not come in on Mondays. Carol served a queue of eleven without once looking at the till longer than she needed to.

On Tuesday he found the permission slip for the school trip, folded small, in the pocket of the apron she had left on the hook. The amount on it was forty pounds. He stood in the back room holding it for some time before he understood.
