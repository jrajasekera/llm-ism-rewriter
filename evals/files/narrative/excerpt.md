The locksmith's van was still outside when Dov got back from the school run. He sat in the car with the engine off. He was relieved. It was relief he felt, the plain easing that comes when a thing you dreaded has been dealt with by somebody else. Through the windscreen he watched the man fit the new cylinder and test it twice.

Leah came out with two keys on a ring and held one up. He got out of the car. "He says the old one was fine," she said. "He says nobody forced it."

"Then how did they get in?"

She looked at the key in her hand and did not answer, and he saw her decide not to say what she was thinking.
