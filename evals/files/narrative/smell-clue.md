# The Cellar Door

The hallway smelled of gas. It was faint, and Marta noticed it only because she had stopped to take off her boots. Her aunt was in the kitchen with the radio on, slicing onions, and did not look up.

"Has someone been at the boiler?" Marta said.

"Not since March."

Marta went down the cellar steps with her boots still in her hand. The smell was stronger at the bottom. The pilot light was out, and the valve beside it stood a quarter open.
