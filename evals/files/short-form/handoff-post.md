I spent last week reading a year of our on-call notes. And the more I sit with it, the clearer it gets: our worst incidents started at shift handoffs.

Teams that write handoffs get handoffs. Teams that write nothing get nothing. It's that simple.

The fix? A habit. The boring kind. The kind that survives a holiday rota and a reorg.

So what does this mean for engineering leads? Three things. First, make the handoff note mandatory. Second, keep it to one page. Third, read it aloud at the start of each shift.

Good notes, good handoffs. Bad notes, bad handoffs.
