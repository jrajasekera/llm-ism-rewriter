Good evening, everyone.

Ten years ago this program was a folding table and a borrowed van. Tonight it is four hundred volunteers and a waiting list.

The reason? You. The cost? Your Saturdays, your evenings, and more than a few of your holidays.

Those who give an hour remember the hour. Those who give a year remember the year. The volunteers who stay will change this city. The ones who don't will still have changed a family.

So tonight we say thanks. The loud kind. The kind that goes on long enough to embarrass everyone in this room.

Thank you.
