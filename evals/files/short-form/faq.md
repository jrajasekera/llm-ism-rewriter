# Frequently asked questions

## Does the sync client work offline?

Yes. Changes queue locally and upload when the connection returns. The queue holds up to 500 changes; past that, the client asks before discarding the oldest.

## Why was my export cancelled?

Exports stop after one hour. Split a large project by folder and export each part separately.

## How do I move my account to a new email address?

Open Settings, choose Account, and enter the new address. We send a confirmation link to both addresses, and the change takes effect when you click either one.

## Is there a free tier?

Yes, for personal projects with up to three collaborators. Teams need a paid plan.

- **The price?** Eight dollars per seat per month, billed yearly.
- **The trial?** Thirty days, with every feature enabled.
